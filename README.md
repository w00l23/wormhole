# Wormhole

A small ship leaves Earth, tears space open, flies through a wormhole and
comes out at Saturn. One HTML file with three.js; the Earth, the stars and
the Milky Way are the real ones.

**Run it:** open `index.html` in a browser. It loads three.js from a CDN,
so it needs a network connection the first time.

Tap or click to start the trip. Steer with the mouse, a finger, the arrow
keys or WASD. The button in the top right switches the sound between
chimes, hum and off. All sound is made in the page; nothing is recorded.

## Time machine

The button in the top left switches to a second story: Kip Thorne's
wormhole time machine (Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever, 1988). Two mouths of
one wormhole wait side by side near Earth, each with a clock. The ship
takes mouth B on a ten-year round trip at nearly the speed of light, and
its clock runs slow by the real factor, gamma = 1 / sqrt(1 - v²/c²). Back
beside A, the two clocks disagree by years. Step in through A and out of B,
and you arrive in B's past: an Earth with other clouds and fewer lights.

The slider at the top sets the ship's top speed, from 50% to 99.9% of
light, and the gap it will open is worked out live from the same formula.
`?mode=time` opens the page on this story.
